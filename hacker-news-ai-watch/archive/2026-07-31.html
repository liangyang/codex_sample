World AI News Connection

Today's HN reaction stretches AI evaluation across three layers: model economics, behavior over long-running workflows, and action in physical or security-sensitive environments. As consequences rise, benchmark scores alone explain less; deployment contracts, traces, interventions, and total cost explain more.

The developer projects point to the same conclusion. Skills, SDKs, isolation strategies, and multi-agent consoles should make constraints visible and testable. Abstractions are valuable only when they preserve provider differences, trust boundaries, and enough evidence to reproduce failures.
